# Prompts para Higgsfield: picadura de abeja

Tres ilustraciones del artículo `picadura-abeja-perros-mayores`, listas para pegar. Se derivan de §8 del brief aprobado.

## Cómo generarlas

1. Modelo recomendado: **Nano Banana Pro** (o GPT Image 2), relación **16:9**, resolución **2k**.
2. En «imagen de referencia» sube **`foto-05.jpg`** de `docs/experiencias/dakota-picadura-abeja-2026-11-11/`, para que la beagle se parezca a Dakota.
3. Genera primero la **Imagen 1**. Si te gusta, usa esa imagen como referencia para la 2 y la 3, así las tres muestran la misma perra.
4. Descarga las que elijas y pásamelas. Yo reviso la edad a tamaño móvil, la anatomía (dedos, patas, manos) y que coincidan con el texto, y las convierto a `.webp`.

Los prompts están en inglés porque los modelos lo siguen con más precisión. Debajo de cada uno va qué rechazar.

## Imagen 1 — Portada: revisar la almohadilla

**Va en:** portada (`beagle-senior-pata-almohadilla-revision.webp`).

```text
Editorial documentary photograph, 16:9. A senior female beagle, tricolor (black saddle on the back, head and ears in very faded pale tan, white chest, legs and tail tip), sturdy and slightly overweight, about 20 kg. Visible old age: white-grey muzzle, eyebrows and eyelids, the tan of the head faded and dull; slightly sunken eyes with slightly drooping lids and a calm gaze, no cloudiness; broad back with a softened, slightly sagging topline and less muscle in the hindquarters; calm senior posture, head resting at back level; no limp, wounds, exaggerated swelling or suffering.
Setting: simple Latin American living room at night, terracotta-red clay floor tiles with visible grout, a rectangular orthopedic foam dog bed with beige sherpa cover and dark grey trim lying on the floor, a grey sofa softly out of focus behind, a folded damp cotton cloth next to the bed.
Action: the dog lies on her side on the bed, relaxed, offering one front paw. A man kneels on the floor beside the bed, facing her; his left hand holds the paw gently at the wrist and his right thumb softly parts the fur between the toes to look at one slightly reddened paw pad. No tweezers or sharp objects. The dog looks at him calmly.
Camera at bed height, slightly from above, medium shot with margin around the paw and hands. Warm light from a floor lamp on the left, soft consistent shadows. Textures of sherpa, short dense fur and tile. Medium depth of field: paw, hands and the dog's face sharp.
Natural documentary finish, careful composition, not a stock or catalog photo. No visible bee, no cartoon stinger, no brand logos, not an American suburban house.
Reject if at phone size the dog looks like a young adult.
```

**Rechazar si:** dedos de más o fusionados, pata con articulación imposible, almohadillas mal colocadas, abeja visible, perra asustada, o si no parece vieja (canas, ojos, lomo).

## Imagen 2 — Sacar el aguijón con una tarjeta

**Va en:** «Saca el aguijón cuanto antes» (`beagle-senior-retirar-aguijon-abeja-tarjeta.webp`).

```text
Editorial documentary photograph, 16:9, wide detail shot at 45 degrees. The same senior female beagle, tricolor (black saddle, very faded pale tan head and ears, white chest, legs and tail tip), sturdy, about 20 kg, lying calmly on a beige sherpa orthopedic dog bed on terracotta clay tiles. In the foreground, the white back of one front paw; behind it, slightly out of focus but readable, her resting head and part of her back and hip. Visible old age: white-grey muzzle, eyebrows and eyelids, faded dull tan; slightly sunken eye with drooping lid and calm gaze; broad, slightly sagging topline with less muscle in the hindquarters; calm lying posture; no wounds, blood or exaggerated swelling.
Action: an adult hand holds the paw gently at the wrist; the other hand slides the edge of a plain plastic card with no logos or numbers parallel to the skin, pushing sideways a small dark bee stinger with a tiny venom sac attached. The card touches the fur without digging in.
Soft natural window light from the upper right; shallow-to-medium depth of field focused on the stinger and the card edge, head and back still recognizable. Textures of short white fur and sherpa.
Natural documentary finish, not a stock photo. No tweezers, no blood, no torn skin, no oversized or cartoon stinger, no credit-card branding, no extra fingers.
Reject if at phone size the dog looks like a young adult.
```

**Rechazar si:** el aguijón es enorme, la tarjeta tiene marca o números, hay sangre o pinzas, o no se ven la cabeza y el lomo para comprobar la edad.

## Imagen 3 — Frío y vigilancia

**Va en:** final de «Los 10 minutos que importan» (`beagle-senior-compresa-fria-vigilancia.webp`).

```text
Editorial documentary photograph, 16:9. The same senior female beagle, tricolor (black saddle, very faded pale tan head and ears, white chest, legs and tail tip), sturdy, about 20 kg. Visible old age: white-grey muzzle, eyebrows and eyelids, faded dull tan; slightly sunken eyes with drooping lids and calm gaze, no cloudiness; broad, slightly sagging topline with less muscle in the hindquarters; lying in sphinx position on her bed with her head low and relaxed; face not swollen, no wounds or suffering.
Setting: simple Latin American living room at night, terracotta clay tiles, orthopedic foam dog bed with beige sherpa cover, a lit floor lamp, a smartphone lying face down on the floor next to the man. No cables or electrical devices near the damp cloth.
Action: a man sits on the floor beside the bed, facing the dog; with one hand he holds a folded damp cool cotton cloth over her front paw (no ice touching the skin), with the other he strokes her chest while watching her face. The dog is awake, calm, looking at him.
Camera at floor level, medium side shot with margin around both. Warm lamp light from the right and a touch of cool night light from a window; consistent shadows; medium depth of field.
Natural documentary finish, careful composition, not a stock photo. No ice cubes on skin, no swollen face or muzzle, no towel over the head, no brand logos.
Reject if at phone size the dog looks like a young adult.
```

**Rechazar si:** hay hielo directo, cara o hocico hinchados, cables junto al paño húmedo, o no parece vieja.
