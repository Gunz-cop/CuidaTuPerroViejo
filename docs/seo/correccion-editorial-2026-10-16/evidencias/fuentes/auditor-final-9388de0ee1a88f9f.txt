Estrogens are widely distributed and are generally found in higher concentrations in sex hormone target organs. Estriol is conjugated in the liver and excreted via urine.       downloadContent TopicPara_topicPara__06ZHa TopicPara_inlineSpacingFix__tKAA9 para" id="v86108913" data-testid="topicPara"><span data-testid="topicText" class="TopicPara_topicText__G6F4C">Adverse effects include anorexia, emesis, polydipsia, anxiety, aggression, lethargy, hypersalivation, swollen vulvae, estrous behavior, sexual attractiveness, and mammary hyperplasia. Polyuria and polydipsia, pregnancy, lactation, and age < 1 year are contraindications. </span></p></div></section><span id="alpha-adrenergic-agonists" class="TopicFHead_topicFHeadSectionWithoutScroll__v86E8" tabindex="-1" data-testid="topicFHeadAnchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><span id="v86108914" class="TopicFHead_topicFHeadSectionWithoutScroll__v86E8" tabindex="-1" data-testid="topicFHeadAnchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><span id="v86108915" class="TopicFHead_topicFHeadSectionWithoutScroll__v86E8" tabindex="-1" data-testid="topicFHeadAnchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><section id="Alpha-Adrenergic-Agonists_v86108914" class=" veterinary TopicFHead_topicFHeadSectionIdWithoutScroll__68joZ"><h2 class="TopicFHead_topicFHead___lHdE
          veterinary sticky-fhead readable downloadContent  undefined" data-originaltitle="Alpha-Adrenergic Agonists" data-testid="topicFHeadHeading"><span class="TopicFHead_topicFHeadTitle__6uLUl"><span>Alpha-Adrenergic Agonists for Treatment of Urinary Incontinence in Animals</span></span><span class="hidden-span"></span></h2><div class="TopicFHead_fHeadBody__wxFX8" data-testid="fheadbody"><p class=" TopicPara_topicParaWithoutScroll__yzic5 readable

      downloadContent TopicPara_topicPara__06ZHa TopicPara_inlineSpacingFix__tKAA9 para" id="v86108923" data-testid="topicPara"><span data-testid="topicText" class="TopicPara_topicText__G6F4C">Alpha-adrenergic agonist veterinary products approved for use in dogs are now available, including an extended-release tablet for once-daily administration. Adverse effects of alpha-adrenergic agonists include excitability, restlessness, hypertension, and anorexia.</span></p></div></section><span id="testosterone" class="TopicFHead_topicFHeadSectionWithoutScroll__v86E8" tabindex="-1" data-testid="topicFHeadAnchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><span id="v86108924" class="TopicFHead_topicFHeadSectionWithoutScroll__v86E8" tabindex="-1" data-testid="topicFHeadAnchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><span id="v86108925" class="TopicFHead_topicFHeadSectionWithoutScroll__v86E8" tabindex="-1" data-testid="topicFHeadAnchor" aria-hidden="true"></span><section id="Testosterone_v86108924" class=" veterinary TopicFHead_topicFHeadSectionIdWithoutScroll__68joZ"><h2 class="TopicFHead_topicFHead___lHdE
